Who we are

Goa's only Student Go-Kart Club

Ignition Crew is the official student motorsport engineering team of Padre Conceição College of Engineering, Goa, where students design, manufacture, and compete with a high-performance racing go-kart while applying real-world engineering principles such as chassis design, vehicle dynamics, fabrication, and testing.
Through national competitions like the Go-Kart Design Challenge (GKDC), the team represents the institute while gaining practical engineering experience and developing industry-ready skills.
